Responsible Use & Safety

Some third-party apps may involve paid entry, chance-based games or financial risk. Such services may be restricted or unlawful in some locations.

  • Check local laws and age restrictions before using any third-party service.
  • Do not treat games as a source of income or a way to recover losses.
  • Never borrow money or spend funds needed for essentials.
  • Set personal limits and stop if play causes stress or financial difficulty.
  • Seek support from a trusted person if you feel unable to control your use.
