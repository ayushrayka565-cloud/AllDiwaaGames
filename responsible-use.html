Wellbeing and online safety

Responsible Use

Apps should not interfere with your safety, finances, studies, sleep or relationships. This page shares practical steps for recognising risk and protecting yourself online.

Important: This site is an information directory, not a gaming service. We do not encourage deposits, wagering or attempts to recover losses. Age-restricted services should not be accessed by anyone under 18.

Warning Signs

Consider taking a break and seeking support if you notice any of the following:

  • You spend more time or money than you intended, repeatedly.
  • You feel pressure to recover money you have lost.
  • You borrow money, use funds needed for essentials or hide spending.
  • You feel restless, anxious or irritable when you stop.
  • Your sleep, studies, work or relationships are affected.
  • You keep using an app because a reward, bonus or status level feels close.

Healthy Digital Limits

  • Set a clear screen-time limit and use device-level app timers.
  • Do not spend money on chance-based or wagering features.
  • Do not chase losses or borrow money to continue using an app.
  • Avoid apps when distressed, tired or under pressure.
  • Take regular breaks and protect time for sleep, studies, work and relationships.
  • Ask a trusted person to help you stick to limits if needed.

Taking a Break

  • Uninstall the app if you do not need it and review any permissions it had.
  • Use available device controls to restrict app access or installs.
  • Leave groups or channels that pressure you to install apps or spend money.
  • Remove unnecessary saved payment methods from services you no longer use.
  • Tell someone you trust that you are taking a break.
  • If an account or payment issue exists, contact the provider through a verified official channel; never share an OTP or PIN.

Protect Your Money and Data

  • Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, banking password or screen-sharing access.
  • Do not install APK files from unknown messages, unofficial mirrors or social-media groups.
  • Check the developer name, permissions, privacy policy and requested access before installing an app.
  • Be cautious of people selling guaranteed outcomes, secret tricks, hacks or recovery services.
  • Use your device's security updates and screen lock; remove apps you no longer trust.

Getting Support in India

If app use or gaming is causing distress, speak with a trusted adult, doctor, counsellor or qualified mental-health professional. You do not need to wait until a problem becomes severe before asking for help.

India's Tele-MANAS mental-health support service can be reached at 14416 or 1-800-891-4416. For an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services; in India, dial 112.

If you are under 18, talk to a parent, guardian, teacher or another trusted adult. Do not try to manage serious distress alone.

For Parents and Guardians

  • Use age-appropriate parental controls and restrict installs from unknown sources.
  • Keep conversations open about app permissions, in-app purchases, money-related features and online contacts.
  • Review device usage and payment notifications together where appropriate.
  • Seek professional support if app use is affecting a young person's wellbeing.

Legal Awareness

Rules for online games and digital services vary by category and jurisdiction. India's Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 and its 2026 rules address online money games and other categories. Do not assume an app is lawful because it is available through a link or appears in a directory. Read the Legal Information page and consult current official sources.
