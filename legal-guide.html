Online Gaming Law in India

A plain-language starting point for understanding the central online gaming framework. This page is not legal advice and does not classify any individual app.

Read before relying on this page: The legal position can change through notifications, rules and court orders. Confirm the latest position using the official sources linked below or obtain advice from a qualified Indian lawyer.

The Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025

Parliament enacted the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 (Act No. 32 of 2025). The Act contains provisions addressing online money games, advertisements related to online money games and transfers of funds, alongside provisions concerning e-sports, online social games and an online gaming authority.

The Act's commencement is determined by government notification. The official notification appointed 1 May 2026 as the commencement date. The Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Rules, 2026 also state that they come into force on 1 May 2026.

Official sources: Act text in the Gazette of India, 2026 Rules notification, and PIB explanation of the Rules.

Different Types of Online Games

The Act distinguishes online money games from other categories, including e-sports and online social games. Whether a particular service falls within a statutory definition depends on its actual features, payment model and relevant legal interpretation. A label such as “rummy”, “skill”, “social” or “free” does not by itself settle the legal question.

What This Means for App Directories

A directory should not assume that every app with a familiar name has the same legal status. App operators, features, payment flows and access conditions can differ. We do not determine whether any app listed on All Diwaa Games complies with Indian law. Do not use a directory listing as evidence that an app is authorised or lawful.

Access, Payments and Consumer Risk

Regulatory action or service restrictions may affect access to online services. A user may also face difficulty contacting an offshore or unregulated operator or resolving a payment dispute. Do not keep funds in a service you do not understand, and do not assume a displayed balance can always be withdrawn.

Court Proceedings and Legal Updates

Challenges to legislation and later court orders can affect how laws are interpreted or enforced. This page does not provide a live case tracker. Before relying on a claim about a pending challenge, stay order or final judgment, check the current Supreme Court record and official orders.

Practical Steps

  • Read the Act and applicable rules rather than relying on app advertisements or directory pages.
  • Check whether the service and its payment features are permitted where you live.
  • Do not install unknown APK files or share banking credentials.
  • Do not treat app availability, a bonus claim or a successful payment as proof of legality.
  • Seek qualified legal advice if the answer affects a real decision or dispute.

Legal Information FAQs

When did the Act come into force?

The commencement notification appointed 1 May 2026. The Rules also specify 1 May 2026 as their commencement date.

Are all card games or rummy apps treated the same?

Do not assume so. The legal classification depends on the statutory definitions and the actual service. Obtain current legal advice for a specific app or activity.

Does a listing on this website mean an app is legal?

No. A listing is not an approval, licence, legal opinion or endorsement.

Can I rely on this page as legal advice?

No. It is general information with links to official materials.
