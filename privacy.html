Privacy Policy

This policy describes the information that may be processed when you visit All Diwaa Games and how to contact us with a privacy question.

Site-owner check required: This draft must be checked against the actual hosting, analytics, forms, redirects and third-party scripts enabled on the live website before publication.

Information That May Be Processed

Technical and server information

When a website is hosted, the hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP address, browser and device details, requested page, referring page, date and time, and security events. The exact information, retention period and access controls depend on the hosting provider's current practices.

Messages sent to us

If you contact us, we may receive your email address and the information you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, bank credentials or sensitive identity documents.

Site interaction and analytics

Any analytics, click measurement, advertising tags, redirect tracking or embedded services must be disclosed here if they are enabled. This draft does not assume that such tools are present.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Static pages may not require account cookies, but hosting, security, embedded content or third-party services can use cookies or similar technologies. Review the live site configuration and update this section to identify any non-essential analytics or advertising cookies before enabling them.

Third-Party Apps and Websites

Links on this site may take you to third-party websites or app providers. Once you leave our website, the third party's privacy policy and terms apply. We do not control what information those providers collect or how they use it. Review their policies and permissions before sharing information or installing software.

How Information Is Used

Information received through site operations or direct messages may be used to maintain website availability and security, respond to enquiries, investigate reported errors and improve the accuracy of published information. The site owner should update this section if any additional purpose is introduced.

Data Sharing

Information may be processed by the website's hosting or security providers as needed to deliver the site, and may be disclosed where required by law or to protect the site and its visitors. The site owner should identify any other service providers that receive personal information.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable measures appropriate to a small informational website. HTTPS protects information in transit between your browser and the site, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Retention periods depend on the hosting provider and the purpose for which information is held.

Children and Age-Restricted Services

This website is an information directory and does not invite children to use age-restricted services. We do not intentionally request sensitive payment information through this site. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

Your Privacy Requests

You may contact us to ask about a message or personal information you have sent directly to the site. We may need enough information to locate the relevant communication and verify the request. Some technical records may be controlled by the hosting provider rather than the site owner.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the website's features, providers or applicable requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page.
