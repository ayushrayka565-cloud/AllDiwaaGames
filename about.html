About All Diwaa Games

An independent information directory that helps visitors understand the names and publicly available information of apps listed on this website. A listing is not a recommendation, endorsement or guarantee.

About Us

All Diwaa Games is an independent website that organises information about a collection of Android app names in one place. We created the directory to make it easier to identify individual apps and find the information pages associated with them.

App names, logos and descriptions may be supplied by third parties or taken from publicly available material. We do not assume that an app is safe, lawful, reliable or suitable merely because it appears in this directory.

What We Do

  • Maintain a directory of app names and their individual information pages.
  • Provide general explanations about app categories, permissions, privacy and digital safety.
  • Explain common risks associated with third-party downloads and financial features.
  • Review public information and correct clear errors when they are brought to our attention.
  • Provide links to relevant site policies so readers can understand the limits of this directory.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not own, develop, operate or control third-party apps listed here.
  • We do not open user accounts, handle deposits, process withdrawals or hold user funds.
  • We do not independently audit app operators, source code, security, game outcomes or financial records.
  • We do not promise income, prizes, returns, bonuses, successful withdrawals or uninterrupted availability.
  • We do not publish fabricated ratings, testimonials, user counts or download statistics.

How the Site Is Funded

Some links may be supplied by third parties or may have a referral relationship. If a link is compensated, that relationship should be clearly disclosed beside the relevant link or on the page. We do not claim that every link is compensated. A commercial relationship must not be treated as evidence that an app is trustworthy or suitable.

Our Standards

  • Distinguish verified facts from estimates, third-party claims and information we cannot confirm.
  • Do not invent ratings, reviews, download totals or promotional figures.
  • Identify third-party ownership and avoid implying official affiliation.
  • Include relevant privacy, security, age and legal cautions.
  • Correct or remove information when it is materially inaccurate or cannot be substantiated.

Contact and Corrections

If you find a broken link, incorrect app identification, outdated statement or content that should be reviewed, use the Contact page. Please include the page address and a short explanation. Do not send passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs or bank details.
