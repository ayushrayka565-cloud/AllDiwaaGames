How We Review Apps

This page explains how app names and information are selected for the directory, what we can verify, and what we do not claim to know.

Which Apps We List

An app entry should identify a specific app and point to an information page about that app. A third-party download or external link should only be published when its destination and ownership can be reasonably identified. If a link cannot be checked, it should not be presented as an official download link. A listing is not an endorsement.

What We Check

  • App identity: Whether the name and available description consistently identify the same app.
  • Link destination: Whether a supplied external link opens the expected provider or product page.
  • Publicly stated features: We distinguish provider claims from features independently tested by us.
  • Permissions and privacy: We encourage readers to review requested permissions and the provider's privacy policy.
  • Support and terms: We may summarise publicly displayed support channels and terms, but do not verify the quality of support.
  • Legal and safety context: We include general cautions and link to official information where available.

What We Cannot Verify

We generally cannot inspect private systems, source code, random-number generators, operator finances, payment processing, withdrawal records or the fairness of game outcomes. We therefore do not certify that an app is safe, fair, licensed, profitable or guaranteed to pay.

No Invented Numbers

We do not make up star ratings, review counts, player totals, download counts, bonus amounts or testimonials. If a number cannot be tied to a reliable, clearly identified source, it should be omitted or labelled as an unverified third-party claim.

Risk and Legal Information

Pages should identify relevant age, privacy, security and financial risks. A third-party app's presence in this directory does not mean it is lawful in every location. Legal information is general and should link to official sources rather than make unsupported claims about a particular operator.

Updates and Corrections

Links and app details can change. We review information when errors are reported and when we have a reason to recheck it. A page should show a last-reviewed date only when that review actually occurred. Send correction requests through the Contact page.

Referral and Commercial Relationships

If a link is a referral or otherwise compensated, that fact should be disclosed clearly and close to the link. Compensation must not be described as proof of app quality and must not determine whether warnings are included.
